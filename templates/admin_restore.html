Restore

Upload a previous backup JSON. Categories are upserted; missing staff accounts are inserted. Restores categories, missing staff/residents, and complaints by tracking number (skips duplicates). Messages are re-linked when possible. For a full wipe-and-restore, use a PostgreSQL dump.

Full database disaster recovery

JSON export is for portable data. For production recovery of schema, indexes, sequences, and foreign keys, use PostgreSQL: 

pg_dump "$DATABASE_URL" -Fc -f backup.dump
pg_restore -d "$DATABASE_URL" --clean --if-exists backup.dump
