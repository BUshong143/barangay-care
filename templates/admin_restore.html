Restore

Upload a previous backup JSON. Categories are upserted; missing staff accounts are inserted. Complaints are restored by tracking number (skips duplicates). For a full wipe-and-restore, use a PostgreSQL dump.

Full database recovery

JSON export is portable data only. For production recovery of schema and indexes, use PostgreSQL: 

pg_dump "$DATABASE_URL" -Fc -f backup.dump
pg_restore -d "$DATABASE_URL" --clean --if-exists backup.dump
